# NABD — 2–3 minute screen-only demo script

**Recording format:** screen capture with voice-over or captions. No face is needed for this script. Use fictional inputs only; do not show personal health information.

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem
"Health information can be confusing, especially when someone is unsure what to do next. NABD is a youth-focused health-literacy prototype designed to make reliable basic information and early action easier to understand."

## 0:20–0:45 — The homepage
Show the NABD homepage and briefly point out the learning, prevention, and responsible-design sections.

"NABD brings health education, practical habits, accessibility, and guided reflection together in one simple website. It is designed to support awareness, not replace professional care."

## 0:45–1:10 — Accessibility and language
Switch between English and Arabic, then demonstrate larger text or high contrast.

"The prototype includes language options and accessibility controls such as text resizing, high contrast, larger buttons, read-aloud, and keyboard-friendly navigation. Translation coverage still needs fluent human review."

## 1:10–1:55 — Guided demo
Use fictional selections: choose a recent concern and low impact, then submit. Optionally repeat with the serious/worsening checkbox selected to show the caution path.

"The guidance demonstration uses transparent rules based on the answers selected. It does not identify diseases or recommend treatment. If a concern is serious or rapidly worsening, the prototype tells the user not to rely on it and to seek appropriate professional or emergency help."

## 1:55–2:25 — Privacy and limitations
Show the privacy note and safety section.

"This local prototype does not send form answers to a server. The optional note is only displayed back to the user. This version does not contain a real AI model, and it has not been clinically validated."

## 2:25–2:50 — Future direction
Show the roadmap.

"The next steps are expert review, inclusive testing, translation review, and privacy and security assessment. Any future AI feature would need a secure design and careful evaluation. NABD's goal is simple: one step ahead through health awareness and responsible early action."

## Recording checklist
- Keep the video between 2 and 3 minutes.
- Record only the browser window or screen; do not show personal notifications, email, or account details.
- Use fictional answers only.
- Make the text readable and keep the cursor away from important content.
- Check the final video and link before submitting.
